# Global-Sales-Profitability-Dashboard-GitHub-Project-Insight


Project Description

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze global sales performance, profitability, discount impact, and shipping costs across different regions and product categories. The dashboard provides actionable business insights through KPI tracking, trend analysis, and geographic visualizations to support data-driven decision-making.


Global Sales & Profitability Dashboard – GitHub Project Insight

📌 Project Overview

This project presents a Global Sales & Profitability Dashboard developed in Power BI to analyze business performance across different regions, product segments, and time periods.

The dashboard provides interactive insights into:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Shipping Cost Impact
- Regional Profitability
- Discount Impact on Profit
- Sales Trend Analysis
- Geographic Sales Distribution

The goal of this project is to help businesses identify profitable regions, monitor operational costs, and understand how discounts affect overall profitability.

📊 Key Business Insights

1. Strong Overall Business Performance

- Total Sales reached $12.64M
- Total Profit generated was $1.47M
- Overall Profit Margin stands at 12%
- Total Orders processed exceeded 25K

This indicates steady business growth with healthy profitability across global operations.

2. Central Region Leads Profitability

The Central Region generated the highest profit of approximately $0.31M, outperforming all other regions.

Other strong-performing regions include:

- North Region → $0.19M
- North Asia → $0.17M
- South Region → $0.14M
- Central Asia → $0.13M

This suggests that the Central Region is a key revenue and profit driver for the business.

3. Discounts Significantly Impact Profit

The dashboard reveals that higher discounts reduce profitability in some product categories.

- Furniture had the highest discount impact with lower profit generation.
- Technology maintained strong profitability despite discounts.
- Office Supplies also showed stable profit performance.

This insight highlights the importance of optimizing discount strategies to protect profit margins.

4. Shipping Costs Affect Regional Profitability

Shipping costs vary across regions and directly influence profit performance.

- The Central Region recorded both high profit and high shipping costs.
- In the South Region, shipping costs appear relatively high compared to generated profit.

This suggests opportunities for logistics optimization and cost reduction strategies.

5. Positive Sales Growth Trend

The sales trend analysis from 2011–2014 shows:

- Consistent upward growth
- Increasing sales momentum over time
- Seasonal fluctuations with overall positive trajectory

The trendline confirms long-term business expansion and increasing market demand.

6. Global Market Presence

The geographic map visualization demonstrates sales distribution across:

- North America
- South America
- Europe
- Africa
- Asia
- Australia

This indicates a broad international market reach and diversified customer base.

The Global Sales & Profitability Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance across multiple dimensions.
